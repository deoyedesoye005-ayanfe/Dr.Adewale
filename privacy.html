Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you use this website or work with us.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dr Cornelius Adewale (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, fill out a form, or engage our farm strategy and consultation services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, and any message you send through our contact or assessment forms.
  • Newsletter data — email address when you subscribe to receive tips, advice, and offers.
  • Service-related information — details you share about your farm, land, or business when you request a consultation or assessment.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and basic usage data (pages visited, time on site) through standard web analytics if enabled.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries, partnership requests, and consultation bookings.
  • Send newsletters and educational content you have opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Deliver the farm strategy, assessment, or done-for-you services you request.
  • Improve our website, courses, events, and resources.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Newsletter and Marketing

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we will send you emails about farm strategy, events, courses, and related offers. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us at hello@corneliusadewale.com.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only when necessary with:

  • Service providers who help us run the website, email, or payment systems (under confidentiality obligations).
  • Professional partners only when you have asked us to introduce you (for example, a vetted agronomist or farm manager).
  • Authorities if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data as safe as reasonably possible.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Newsletter subscribers remain on our list until they unsubscribe. Contact form and consultation records are retained for a reasonable period to support ongoing communication and service quality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal or contractual limits).
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time.

To exercise these rights, email hello@corneliusadewale.com.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to external websites (for example, event platforms, FarmReady, or social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please read their policies when you leave our site.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at adults and business operators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: hello@corneliusadewale.com
  • Location: Lagos & Ilesa, Nigeria
